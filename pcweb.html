Frequently Asked Questions

Who do we reach out to for recruitment-related queries at SRCC?

For any recruitment-related assistance, you can write to us at placementcell@srcc.du.ac.in. You may also directly connect with our Student Coordinators:

  • Shouraya Aggarwal – +91 62394 48010
  • Sjonum Walia – +91 70873 71515
  • Tanvi Bansal – +91 88824 99332

Our team is readily available to provide end-to-end support throughout the recruitment lifecycle.

What are the available modes of recruitment at SRCC?

SRCC facilitates recruitment through both offline and online (virtual) modes. We provide a seamless and flexible recruitment experience tailored to the preferences of our recruiting partners. Companies are encouraged to choose the mode best suited to their operational needs.

Does SRCC offer PwD recruitment support?

Yes. SRCC is deeply committed to inclusive hiring. The Placement Cell actively facilitates the recruitment of Persons with Disabilities (PwD) through targeted initiatives. We work with recruiters to ensure accessible infrastructure, tailored communication, and candidate support throughout the process.

Are international recruitment opportunities facilitated?

Yes. The Placement Cell welcomes and actively supports international recruitment. Companies looking to offer overseas roles or internships can coordinate with our team to structure virtual hiring processes, conduct pre-placement talks, and ensure a transparent and compliant process aligned with university norms.

What is the academic profile of students at SRCC?

SRCC students are enrolled in flagship programmes, B.Com. (Hons.) and B.A. (Hons.) Economics. Our students maintain a strong academic record, with a rigorous curriculum backed by real-world exposure through live projects, internships, and research activities.

Is there any placement policy or guideline document we should refer to?

Yes, all recruiters are expected to abide by The Recruiter Policy 2025-26. The Recruiter Policy 2025-26 shall lay the groundwork for all undergraduate campus recruitment processes taking place at SRCC this year. The Policy entails various procedural norms that have to be mandatorily complied with by all companies recruiting from SRCC. The document shall be shared at the commencement of the recruitment process.
