Frequently Asked Questions

Does SRCC offer PwD Recruitment?

Yes, SRCC is committed to inclusive recruitment practices. The Placement Cell has launched special PwD (Persons with Disabilities) recruitment initiatives to ensure equal opportunities for all students. We work closely with recruiters to facilitate accessible recruitment processes and provide necessary support to PwD candidates.

What are the available modes of Recruitment?

The Placement Cell, SRCC offers both offline as well as online/virtual recruitment modes, as per circumstances. We provide flexible solutions to accommodate recruiter preferences and ensure seamless recruitment processes regardless of the chosen mode.

What facilities are provided during the recruitment process?

SRCC provides comprehensive facilities including well-equipped auditoriums for presentations, computer labs for online assessments, interview rooms, high-speed internet connectivity, audio-visual equipment, and dedicated support staff. We also offer accommodation assistance for outstation recruiters and ensure all logistical requirements are met efficiently.

Who do we reach out to?

For any queries or recruitment-related assistance, please contact our team at placementcell@srcc.du.ac.in or reach out to our student coordinators: Shouraya Aggarwal (+91 6239 448 010), Sjonum Walia (+91 70873 71515), or Tanvi Bansal (+91 88824 99332). Our team is available to provide comprehensive support throughout the recruitment process.
